Live / three proposals
NETWORK MONITOR / DESIGN EXPLORATION

A quieter window into your connection.

Three directions, each with a Live tab and a Recordings tab. Switch between them using the bar below or the left and right arrow keys. You can choose a different direction for each tab.

A / Quiet
A light, spacious dashboard. Connection health comes first; recordings pair a familiar list with a clear route map.
B / Instrument
A compact dark workspace. A larger plot and a persistent diagnostics rail keep detailed observations close at hand.
C / Fieldnotes
A warm travel journal. Larger typography, route context, and a map-led recording view give each journey room to breathe.

These are interactive HTML proposals with illustrative data and locally drawn maps. They do not connect to the monitor. The selected design will be implemented in Leptos and served locally while the monitor runs.

Map colors describe sampled connection quality along a route. They do not predict area or carrier coverage. Dashed sections indicate unavailable location; down/up values are passive interface traffic.
